# Qidi Q2 Superuser — Wiki

Everything you need to get more out of your Qidi Q2 or Qidi Max 4. The All-in-One Installer sets up faster macros, an optional modern touchscreen UI, multi-material support, and a full backup/restore safety net — all from a single SSH command, with no hand-editing config files.
